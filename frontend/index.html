Easy Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients

  • 9–12 lasagna noodles (or oven-ready noodles)
  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) pasta sauce
  • 1–2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, or Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook noodles
    • Boil according to package
    • Drain and set aside
  2. Cook meat
    • Brown the beef
    • Drain fat
    • Add sauce and simmer 5–10 minutes
  3. Mix cheese layer
    • Combine ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper
  4. Layer everything
    • Sauce on bottom
    • Noodles
    • Ricotta mix
    • Meat sauce
    • Mozzarella
    • Repeat 2–3 times
  5. Top it off
    • Add mozzarella
    • Sprinkle parmesan
  6. Bake
    • Cover with foil
    • Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes
    • Remove foil and bake 10–15 more minutes
  7. Let it rest before cutting
