Easy Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients (basic version):

  • 9–12 lasagna noodles (or oven-ready noodles to make it easier)
  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef (or skip for veggie)
  • 1 jar (24 oz) pasta sauce
  • 1–2 cups ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg (optional, helps ricotta stay firm)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, or Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook noodles (skip if oven-ready):

    Boil them according to the package, then drain.

  2. Cook meat:

    Brown the ground beef in a pan, drain fat, then add pasta sauce. Simmer 5–10 minutes.

  3. Mix cheese layer:

    In a bowl, combine ricotta, egg (optional), and a little salt/pepper.

  4. Layer everything (in a baking dish):
    • Sauce on bottom (prevents sticking)
    • Noodles
    • Ricotta mix
    • Meat sauce
    • Mozzarella
    • Repeat layers 2–3 times.

    Repeat layers 2–3 times

  5. Top it off

    Add remaining mozzarella and sprinkle parmesan on top.

  6. Bake:
    • Cover with foil
    • Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes
    • Remove foil, bake another 10–15 minutes until bubbly
    • Rest before cutting
